Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 28, 2026

Calcora is designed to work without requiring an account. Calculator inputs are processed in your browser for the calculations provided by the site. We do not ask you to submit personal information merely to use a calculator.

Information collected automatically

Like most websites, the hosting provider and other technical services may process information such as IP address, browser type, device information, requested pages and timestamps for security, reliability and analytics purposes. The exact information available depends on the services enabled on the site.

Cookies and advertising

Calcora may use cookies or similar technologies if analytics or advertising services are added in the future. If Google AdSense or another advertising service is enabled, that provider may use cookies and similar technologies to serve or measure advertising in accordance with its own policies.

Third-party services

The site is hosted using GitHub Pages. If third-party analytics, advertising, consent-management or other services are added, their processing will be governed by their own terms and privacy policies as well as the disclosures applicable to Calcora.

Your choices

You can control cookies through your browser settings. Where legally required, Calcora will provide appropriate consent choices before using non-essential cookies or similar technologies.

Contact

For privacy questions or requests concerning the site, use the contact information provided on the Contact page.
