About Calcora

Calcora is a free collection of simple online calculators designed to help people make everyday calculations quickly and understand the assumptions behind each result.

What we provide

Our tools cover areas such as car costs, income, tips, dates, GPA and everyday math. Most calculations run directly in your browser, so you can use the tools without creating an account.

How the calculators work

Each calculator uses the inputs shown on its page and a clearly described formula or assumption. Results are estimates and can differ from figures provided by lenders, employers, schools, government agencies or other official sources.

Our approach

We aim for simple interfaces, readable explanations and transparent assumptions. We do not promise that a calculator is suitable for every individual situation.

Important information

Calcora does not provide financial, tax, legal, medical or other professional advice. For decisions with significant consequences, verify important information with an appropriate qualified professional or official source.
